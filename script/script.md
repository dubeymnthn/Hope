**Purpose**: *Explain the 3x silicon penalty of HBM vs standard DDR5*

> **Narration**: "To feed modern AI processors, manufacturers must use High Bandwidth Memory. But here is the catch: producing one gigabyte of HBM takes three times as much physical silicon wafer area as standard DDR5."

### Scene 4: scene-004
**Purpose**: *Highlight the CoWoS packaging choke point at TSMC*
